Our Services

Empowering members through collective action and financial growth

What We Offer

BODMAS Chamaa provides structured services designed to empower members financially, socially, and economically through teamwork, discipline, and accountability.

Member Savings Program

Structured savings plans that promote financial discipline and collective capital growth.

Loan & Credit Support

Accessible loans for eligible members to support business growth, emergencies, and personal development.

Investment Opportunities

Group-based investments that generate returns and strengthen member wealth.

Financial Literacy Training

Workshops and seminars on savings culture, budgeting, entrepreneurship, and responsible investment.

Community Support Programs

Welfare assistance and community empowerment initiatives for vulnerable individuals and families.

Youth, Sports & Talent Development

Supporting youth initiatives, sports activities, and nurturing local talents as part of social responsibility.

Our Service Principles

  • Transparency and accountability
  • Member-centered decision making
  • Financial discipline and responsibility
  • Collective growth and shared success
  • Compliance with Kenyan laws and regulations
