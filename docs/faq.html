Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about BODMAS CHAMAA and how the group works.

Membership

How do I join?

Submit a membership application through the contact page, attend an introduction session, and complete the registration process.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. A one-time registration fee applies, as approved by the group.

Savings

How much is the monthly contribution?

The monthly contribution amount is set by the group and communicated to all members.

What happens if I miss a contribution?

Late contributions may attract penalties per the group's policies.

Loans

How do I qualify for a loan?

You must be an active member in good standing with demonstrated repayment capacity and qualified guarantors.

What is the loan interest rate?

Interest is disclosed on approval based on current group policy.

Investments

How are returns paid?

Returns are paid based on the performance of the specific investment program as approved by the group.

Still have questions?

Reach out and we'll be happy to help.
