Loans

Accessible credit for members — built on discipline, trust, and accountability.

Loan Eligibility

  • Active member in good standing
  • Minimum contribution period as set by the group
  • Demonstrated repayment capacity
  • Qualified guarantors
  • No outstanding defaults

Application Process

1. Apply

Submit a formal loan application.

2. Review

Committee reviews eligibility and capacity.

3. Guarantors

Provide qualified member guarantors.

4. Approval

Loan approved and disbursed per policy.

Interest & Repayment

Interest is charged on agreed terms disclosed before disbursement. Repayments are made in scheduled installments with clearly defined due dates. Late payments may attract penalties per the group's policy.

Default Handling

In case of default, the group follows the constitution's procedures, including engaging guarantors and applying prescribed recovery measures. Members are encouraged to communicate early if repayment is difficult.

Full loan accounts, schedules, and statements are available inside the Member Portal.
