Member Savings Program

Building financial discipline and collective growth

Why Save with BODMAS Chamaa?

BODMAS Chamaa promotes a strong saving culture to empower members financially and ensure sustainable growth of the group.

  • Encourages financial discipline
  • Builds capital for loans and projects
  • Supports members during emergencies
  • Promotes long-term financial stability

Our Savings Categories

Regular Monthly Savings

All members are required to contribute a fixed amount monthly as agreed during official chamaa meetings. This forms the main savings pool.

Voluntary Savings

Members are encouraged to save more than the minimum required amount based on their financial ability.

Emergency Savings

These savings are reserved strictly for emergencies and are accessed under approved conditions.

How Our Savings System Works

  1. Members deposit their savings on a monthly basis
  2. All contributions are properly recorded
  3. Funds are securely managed by chamaa officials
  4. Savings determine eligibility for loans and benefits

Savings Rules & Guidelines

  • Members must pay savings on time
  • Late payments may attract penalties
  • Savings are non-refundable while membership is active
  • Transparency and accountability are strictly observed
  • All members must adhere to the chamaa constitution

Benefits of Saving with Us

  • Eligibility for loans
  • Emergency financial support
  • Participation in group investments
  • Financial empowerment and growth
