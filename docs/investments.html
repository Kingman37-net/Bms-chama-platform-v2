Investments

Strategic collective investment for long-term member wealth.

Investment Overview

BODMAS CHAMAA operates structured investment programs aimed at growing member savings while managing risk responsibly. All investments are approved through leadership and overseen by the Board of Directors.

Investment Strategy

Diversification

Spreading investments across sectors to reduce risk.

Due Diligence

Every opportunity is researched and reviewed before approval.

Member Participation

Members are informed and consulted on major investment decisions.

Investment Returns

  • 7.5% return on six (6) month investment programs
  • 15% return on annual investment programs
  • Returns are subject to review based on performance

Risk Disclosure

All investments involve risk. Returns are not guaranteed and depend on business performance and market conditions. Members are advised to understand the investment terms before participation.

Oversight & Accountability

All investment activities are managed exclusively by authorized officials and the Board of Directors. Regular reports are prepared to ensure transparency and accountability.
