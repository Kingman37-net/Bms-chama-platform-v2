BODMAS Chamaa values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you visit our website or become a member.

We may collect the following types of information:

Your information is used to:

Member information is retained for as long as necessary to fulfill operational and legal obligations.
