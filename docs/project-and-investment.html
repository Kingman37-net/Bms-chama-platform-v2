Projects & Investments

Investment Overview

BODMAS CHAMAA operates structured investment programs aimed at growing member savings while managing risk responsibly. All projects and investments are approved through leadership and overseen by the Board of Directors.

Ongoing Projects

Income Generating Ventures

Investments in small and medium-scale businesses designed to generate steady income for members.

Agribusiness Initiatives

Farming and agribusiness projects supporting food security and sustainable returns.

Real Estate & Property

Property acquisition and development for long-term asset growth.

Completed Projects

Successfully completed projects demonstrate the Chamaa’s commitment to transparency, accountability, and prudent financial management. Detailed reports are available to members through official channels.

Upcoming / Proposed Projects

Expansion Programs

Scaling existing projects to increase returns and member participation.

New Investment Opportunities

Exploring new sectors based on market research and member input.

Community-Based Ventures

Projects that combine profitability with social impact.

Investment Returns

  • 7.5% return on six (6) month investment programs
  • 15% return on annual investment programs
  • Returns are subject to review based on performance

Risk Disclosure

All investments involve risk. Returns are not guaranteed and depend on business performance and market conditions. Members are advised to understand the investment terms before participation.

Oversight & Accountability

All investment activities are managed exclusively by authorized officials and the Board of Directors. Regular reports are prepared to ensure transparency and accountability.
