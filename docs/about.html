Who We Are

BODMAS CHAMAA is a registered Self-Help Group operating under the laws of the Republic of Kenya. Founded in January 2023 in Bamburi, Mombasa, the Chamaa was established to promote disciplined saving, collective investment, and community-driven economic empowerment.

Today, BODMAS CHAMAA brings together over 450 active members, operating with transparency, accountability, and a clear digital-first financial management approach as we transition toward a Mini-SACCO structure.

Leadership

George Odongo

Chairperson & Founder

George Odongo is the founding Chairperson of BODMAS CHAMAA and provides strategic leadership focused on financial discipline, institutional growth, and long-term sustainability.

Under his leadership, the Chamaa has grown from a small community group into a structured financial organization with strong governance, digital systems, and a clear roadmap toward Mini-SACCO status.
