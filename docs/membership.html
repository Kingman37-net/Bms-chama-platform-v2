Membership

Join a community committed to savings, investments, and shared prosperity.

Who Can Join

BODMAS CHAMAA welcomes individuals who share our values of discipline, trust, transparency, and long-term financial growth. Membership is open to residents of Mombasa and beyond who are willing to commit to our savings and investment culture.

Membership Requirements

Identification

National ID or valid identification document.

Registration Fee

One-time registration fee as approved by the group.

Monthly Savings

Commitment to monthly contributions per the group's schedule.

Good Standing

Willingness to follow the group constitution and policies.

Joining Process

  • Submit an application for membership
  • Attend an introduction session
  • Pay the registration fee
  • Sign the membership agreement
  • Begin monthly contributions

Member Rights & Responsibilities

Rights

Vote on group matters, access personal records, receive dividends, participate in meetings and elections.

Responsibilities

Attend meetings, contribute on time, respect the constitution, uphold transparency and protect group assets.
